How it works

From a bank statement to a resolved complaint

Pension Helper does the checking. People make the decisions.

  1. UploadThe office uploads the pensioner list and bank statements.
  2. Check rulesDeadlines, DA rates and every month's payment are checked.
  3. AI drafts letterClaude writes a clear, polite complaint to the bank.
  4. Office approvesA person reads and approves every letter before it goes out.
  5. Family informedFamilies follow the status in Hindi or English, no account needed.
Why it helps

Built for busy pension offices

Verify once

Save the Jeevan Pramaan ID once and the pensioner shows “Verified until” for the whole year. No repeated reminders, no repeated questions.

Check payments in minutes

Upload bank statements (CSV or PDF). Every month's pension is compared with what should have been paid, including the latest DA rates.

Guide families to resolution

When money is missing, AI drafts a clear letter to the bank. The office approves it and families follow every step until it is resolved, in Hindi or English.
